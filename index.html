WRITE YOUR FUTURE!

Our Goal

A fact many people in more developed countries do not realise is that there are millions upon millions of children who aren't in a situation that allows for them to reach their greatest potential. Specifically, in countries such as Bolivia in South America, the illiteracy rate is over 10% as many in favelas aren't given the option to attend school, which limits their possibilities and crushes dreams. But, we all can change that. We are looking to provide a charitable service that will give children in South America the chance to learn Portugese, Spanish and English. This will give the children a chance to progress further into education or finding work/work experiences.


What is the plan to make this idea into reality?

For a one-time payment or for a subscription service of once a month to an amount of your choice, you can pay for professional tutors who are willing to join our program and help give classes to the young children in our program.
The money you provide will also allow for us to invest in classrooms, we are looking to purchase small units that could be set up in remote areas that will allow for upwards of ten students at a time. We also wish to look into funding for supplies such as writing paperbacks and pens and pencils that the students will be allowed to keep to do their homework comfortably.
